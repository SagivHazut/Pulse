## The short version

Pulse Blocks does not ask who you are. There is no account, no sign-in, and no
server that stores your progress. Your scores, coins and settings live on your
device and nowhere else.

The one exception is advertising. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and
Google collects some information to deliver and measure them. That is described
in full below.

## What stays on your device

The following is saved locally and never transmitted:

- Your high scores for each mode, and your saved game in progress
- Coins, level, XP and unlocked achievements
- Unlocked themes and block styles, and which one is active
- Your sound, music, haptics and reduced-motion preferences
- Daily reward streak and the date you last claimed

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup and no way for us to
recover it, because we never receive it.

## Advertising

Pulse Blocks shows rewarded ads (which you choose to watch, in exchange for an
in-game reward) and may show ads between games. These are served by **Google
AdMob**.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

- A device advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android, or the
  Identifier for Advertisers on iOS)
- Device information such as model, operating system version and language
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Information about ads shown, including views and interactions

We do not receive this information, and we cannot connect it to you. How Google
uses it is described in **Google's Privacy & Terms**
(https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in
**How Google uses information from partner sites and apps**
(https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### Your choices about advertising

- **In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom**, we ask for your
  consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging
  Platform. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings → Ad
  preferences** inside the game.
- **On iOS**, the system asks separately for permission to track you across apps.
  If you decline, the game works exactly the same and you will still see ads —
  they will simply be less relevant.
- **On Android**, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in
  Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Declining any of these never limits the game. Every feature, including rewarded
ads, remains available.

## Analytics

Pulse Blocks does not use any third-party analytics service. Gameplay events are
written to a local development log only, and are not transmitted anywhere.

## Payments

There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. The game never asks for
payment details.

## Children

Pulse Blocks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. Ad content is limited to a rating
suitable for teens and above. If you believe a child has provided personal
information, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

## Permissions

On Android the game requests only what it needs to function: internet access (to
load ads), vibration (for haptic feedback) and audio settings (for sound
effects). It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts,
photos, files or location.
